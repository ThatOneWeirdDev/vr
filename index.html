Wearing it

  1. Load a video and set the zoom. More zoom means more of the video sits outside your view, waiting for you to turn toward it.
  2. Tap Start VR and turn your phone sideways. On iPhone, tap Allow when it asks about motion.
  3. Hold the phone right in front of your eyes and look straight ahead while it counts down from 3.
  4. Strap or tape it on. A headband with the phone tucked under it works too.
