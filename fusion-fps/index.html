Live camera. It finds moving objects, and when one suddenly stops, like a hand hitting a leg, it is cut out, moved and squashed like a cartoon while the background behind it is filled in from a learned clean plate, and the spot it hit wobbles. Tap anything on the live image to bounce it on demand, or try the demo scene without a camera. Start with the object out of frame and keep the phone still so the background can be learned. Objects are found by motion only, not by recognizing what they are.
